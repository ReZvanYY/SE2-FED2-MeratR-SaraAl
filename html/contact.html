BidSmart is a trusted, full-service auction company established in 2000, proudly celebrating 25 years of industry experience. Built on a foundation of integrity, innovation, and customer-focused service, BidSmart has become a leading name in the auction world—connecting buyers and sellers through transparent, efficient, and results-driven solutions.

Over the past two decades, BidSmart has refined the art of competitive bidding, offering both live and online auction services across a wide range of categories, including estate assets, real estate, equipment, vehicles, collectibles, and commercial liquidations. Our seasoned team combines deep market knowledge with cutting-edge technology to deliver maximum value and a seamless auction experience.

At BidSmart, our mission is simple: make smart bidding accessible for everyone. Whether you're selling a single item or managing a large-scale liquidation, we are committed to providing professional guidance, strategic marketing, and exceptional service every step of the way.
